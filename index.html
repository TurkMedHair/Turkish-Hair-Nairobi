Sub-Dermal Extraction Mechanics

Afro-Curved Root Anatomy & Extraction Science

Understanding the sub-dermal physiological curvature of African hair and how our specialized flared trumpet punches prevent root transection.

Anatomical Trajectory

1. Sub-Dermal Helical Curvature

Unlike straight hair follicles that descend vertically into the scalp at predictable angles, Afro-textured hair curls beneath the skin surface in a distinct helical, C-shaped, or S-shaped trajectory. Standard straight punches cut directly across this sub-dermal bend, causing high transection rates.

Curvature Depth 3.5mm - 4.5mm
Transection Risk Minimized to <2%
Flared Extraction Punch

2. Flared Trumpet Envelopment

Our specialized microscopic trumpet punches feature an outward-tapered tip design (0.9mm - 1.1mm diameter). As the tool rotates gently around the follicle entry point, the outward curve safely guides the sharp cutting edge away from the fragile bulb tissue, surrounding the root safely for 100% intact extraction.

Punch Diameter 0.9mm - 1.1mm
