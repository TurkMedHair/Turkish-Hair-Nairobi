Afro-Texture Specialization

Curved Follicle Micro-Punch Surgery for African Hair

Unlike straight hair follicles that travel vertically into the scalp, Afro-textured hair curls beneath the skin surface in a "C" or "S" shape. Using standard straight FUE punches leads to graft shearing.

01

Flared Trumpet Punches (0.9mm - 1.1mm)

Outwardly curved tip edges accommodate the curly bulb without cutting root tissue.

02

Controlled Sub-Dermal Oscillation

Adjusted rotational speed tailored to dense dermal skin tissue layers prevalent in African scalps.

03

Anti-Keloid Protocol Integrated

Customized post-op care preventing hypertrophic scars and ensuring smooth healing.
