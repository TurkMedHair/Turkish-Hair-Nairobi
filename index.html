Restoration Protocols

Advanced Technical Implementations

Surgical approaches tailored to density needs, scalp geometry, and aesthetic hairline goals.

Protocol 01

Sapphire FUE Hair Transplant

Utilizes ultra-precise microscopic blades crafted from synthetic sapphire stone to construct structural micro-channels. Reduces scabbing, protects surrounding tissue, and ensures dense graft placement.

Inquire Sapphire Plan
Protocol 02

DHI (Direct Hair Implantation)

Harvested follicles are loaded directly into specialized Choi implanter pens. Allows simultaneous channel creation and graft insertion without shaving recipient areas, yielding supreme angle precision.

Inquire DHI Plan
Protocol 03

Beard & Facial Hair Transplant

Harvests robust donor grafts from the scalp lower occipital zone and re-allocates them into patchy facial beard or mustache regions with exact directional growth alignment.

Inquire Beard Plan
Protocol 04

PRP Root Activation Therapy

Autologous concentrated platelet-rich plasma enriched with growth factors injected directly into thinning scalp areas to awaken dormant roots and accelerate surgical recovery.

Inquire PRP Plan
