Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the hair transplant procedure painful?

No. The entire procedure is performed under local anesthesia. Painless needle-free jet injection systems are used prior to local numbing to ensure maximum patient comfort throughout the session.

How long does recovery take after the procedure?

Initial scab shedding occurs within 10 to 12 days post-op. Most patients return to desk work within 3 to 4 days. Full athletic activities can resume after 3 weeks.

When will I see final hair growth results?

New hair growth begins around month 3 to 4. Visible density appears by month 6, with full maturity and final density achieved between 12 and 14 months.

Are the results permanent?

Yes! Hair follicles harvested from the donor zone (back and sides of the head) are genetically resistant to DHT-induced loss, ensuring lifelong natural growth.
