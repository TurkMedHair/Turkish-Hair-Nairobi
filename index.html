Restoration Protocols

Advanced Technical Implementations

Surgical approaches tailored to density requirements, donor capacity, and scalp geometry.

Protocol 01

Sapphire FUE Hair Transplant

Employs microscopic blades crafted from synthetic sapphire stone to form micro-channels. Minimizes scabbing, preserves scalp vascularity, and allows dense graft positioning.

Inquire Sapphire Plan
Protocol 02

DHI (Direct Hair Implantation)

Follicles are loaded directly into specialized Choi implanter pens. Enables simultaneous channel opening and graft insertion without shaving recipient areas, yielding supreme angle control.

Inquire DHI Plan
Protocol 03

Beard & Facial Hair Transplant

Extracts healthy donor grafts from the lower occipital zone and re-allocates them into sparse facial beard or mustache regions with precise directional alignment.

Inquire Beard Plan
Protocol 04

PRP Root Activation Therapy

Concentrated autologous platelet-rich plasma enriched with growth factors injected directly into thinning areas to stimulate dormant roots and boost surgical recovery speed.

Inquire PRP Plan
