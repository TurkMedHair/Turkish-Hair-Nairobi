Restoration Protocols

Advanced Technical Implementations

Sapphire FUE Hair Transplant

Utilizes ultra-precise microscopic blades crafted from synthetic sapphire stone to construct structural micro-channels. Reduces scabbing, protects surrounding tissue, and ensures dense graft placement.
